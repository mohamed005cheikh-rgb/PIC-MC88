# PIC by MC88 

A professional, zero-server image processing studio that runs entirely in your browser.  
100% client-side. Your photos never leave your device.

---

### ✨ Why use it?
Fast, secure, and private. No downloads, no sign-ups, no data leaks.

---

### 🔧 Key Features
- **🔒 Absolute Privacy:** All processing happens locally. Zero uploads.
- **🎨 100+ Artistic Filters:** From vintage restoration to cinematic effects.
- **🧠 AI Restore:** One-click neural enhancement for faded or blurry photos.
- **↔️ Before/After Slider:** Drag to see the difference instantly.
- **💾 Multi-Format Export:** JPEG, PNG, WebP with full quality control.
- **↩️ Undo/Redo:** Full history with keyboard shortcuts.
- **📱 Fully Responsive:** Works seamlessly on mobile and desktop.
- **🎭 3 Themes:** Midnight, Gray, and Light.
- **⭐ Custom Presets:** Save your favorite filter settings for later.

---

### 🚀 Quick Start
1. Open the site.
2. Drop or select your image.
3. Apply filters and adjustments.
4. Download your masterpiece.

**No signup. No servers. Just you and your image.**
